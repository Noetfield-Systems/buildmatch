Before you begin

Common questions

Do I need an account?

No account to use the calculator, create a draft or open a customer review link. Save your private management URL.

Is this a full construction ERP?

No. It is a narrow extra-work → price → approval workflow built to coexist with the tools your office already uses.

Does this guarantee contract approval?

No. Written decisions are recorded; enforceability and authorization depend on your contract, applicable law and the signer's actual authority.

Can my office print the pack?

Yes. Use Print / Save PDF for the free draft and for a paid pack after customer response.
