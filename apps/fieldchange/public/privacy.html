Privacy notice

Last updated: November 10, 2026. BuildWorkflow FieldChange is operated by Noetfield Systems Inc., Canada.

Free pricing calculator

The free pricing API receives the numerical costs and margin settings needed to calculate a quotation. Draft details such as job name, scope and customer contact remain in browser local storage until you choose to create a shareable order. Local drafts are not automatically synchronized.

Shareable change-order records

When you create a pack, BuildWorkflow stores the project, job number, change scope, customer and requester names if supplied, schedule impact, evidence checklist selections, pricing figures and creation time in a restricted application database. The customer review link allows its holder to view the proposed scope and total, but not your internal costs or profit margin. The private contractor management link grants broader access. Treat both links as confidential. Approval-link secrets are encrypted in storage, while access-token hashes are retained for authentication.

Written customer decisions

A reviewer may submit their stated name, approval or decline decision, a note and decision time. A one-way hash derived from request network information is retained as a limited abuse/integrity signal. The system does not perform independent identity verification. We retain historical decisions for audit and dispute resolution; a refund may disable new access without erasing a prior decision.

Stripe and payment records

Paid packs use Stripe-hosted Checkout when enabled. Payment details are submitted directly to Stripe and are subject to Stripe's privacy policy. The application processes signed payment webhooks to store the payment session reference, transaction reference, payment status and activation time. It does not store full payment-card details.

Pilot requests

If you submit a pilot inquiry, we store business contact details, requested trade, current workflow, volume range, optional issue notes and consent. These records are used for follow-up and limited service improvement. A bounded status event may be sent to Noetfield's internal Company OS; it is not used to fabricate customer interest or payment events.

Storage, retention and sharing

Production data is stored on a persistent Railway service volume; you should keep independent copies of important work records. Personal information is used to provide the requested service, manage payment, prevent misuse and support legitimate audit obligations. We do not sell customer data. Hosting and payment providers process data as service providers. Records are retained as long as reasonably necessary for the service, legal, bookkeeping or dispute-resolution purposes.

Access and deletion

For access, correction or deletion requests, contact the merchant using your Stripe receipt or the BuildWorkflow business contact with whom you corresponded. Certain paid or approved records may need to be preserved for legal, financial or dispute reasons. Avoid uploading unnecessary sensitive information or personal details about third parties.

Scope

BuildWorkflow is an operational document workflow, not an identity-verification service, licensed legal practice, accounting or tax-filing provider. See Terms of Service.
