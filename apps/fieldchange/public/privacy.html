Privacy notice — pilot release

Last updated: November 5, 2026.

What is stored

When you request a pilot conversation, BuildWorkflow stores your company, contact name, trade, email, optional phone, selected ticket-volume range, current process, optional problem description, consent, and submission time to assess and respond to the request.

What stays in your browser

Job name, job number, customer name, scope notes, pricing inputs, evidence checklist, and saved drafts remain in local browser storage in this pilot release. The pricing endpoint receives only numeric cost inputs and returns a calculation.

Access and use

Pilot-request information is used for pilot qualification and follow-up and is not sold. Access is restricted to the private pilot administration console.

Retention and deletion

Requests are retained only as needed to assess and follow up on the pilot. A request can be removed from the private admin console. Do not submit confidential project or third-party personal information.

Boundaries

This early pilot does not provide e-signature, contract interpretation, legal advice, accounting or tax filing.

Questions

For correction or deletion, reply to the BuildWorkflow representative who shared the pilot link.
